# Credits

The demo images in [`docs/images`](images) were made by running `gls` on the six pictures below and drawing the
raw terminal output of `gls` (including its Kitty graphics, half-block and text output) onto a terminal-like canvas.
They are **not** photographs of a real terminal window.

All six source pictures are marked **Public domain** on Wikimedia Commons. They are *not* stored in this repository;
only the resulting demo images are.
